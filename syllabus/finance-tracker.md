## Applied statistics add-ons

Folded into existing weeks rather than added as new ones. Each adds ~1 h to that week's build. Assumes stats theory is already covered by the AI Master's; the focus is applying it to business data.

- **S1 — Week 17 (cohorts):** Add confidence intervals to the 24-month cohort decay table. Show how wide the retention range is for a small cohort vs a large one, and write two sentences on why a blended churn rate from small cohorts is unreliable.
- **S2 — Week 18 (pricing):** Design a price test (A/B or before/after) for the repriced product: hypothesis, sample size needed to detect the expected effect, and how you'd tell a real elasticity signal from noise.
- **S3 — Week 21 (forecasting):** Fit a simple regression for one revenue driver (e.g. revenue vs a volume or seasonality driver). Report the fit, the residuals, and a forecast range rather than a single number. Compare it with the driver-based forecast.
- **S4 — Week 37 (downside):** Monte Carlo the Week 27 DCF: put distributions on 3–4 key inputs (growth, margin, discount rate, terminal growth), run ≥1,000 simulations, and produce a value distribution with P10 / P50 / P90. State the probability your chosen price loses money.

Watch-outs to apply throughout: averages hiding skew (median vs mean), survivorship bias in comparables, small-sample ratios, and correlation passed off as cause.
